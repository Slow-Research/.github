# Slow Research

**The Slow Research Efficiency Working Group**

### Make Slow Research Faster! 🚀🚀🚀

**English** · [简体中文](https://github.com/Slow-Research/.github/blob/main/profile/README.zh-CN.md)

We study how to do slow research efficiently. Through better tools, shared methods, and collaboration, we aim to fit more careful thinking into the time we have.

Our research agenda includes slow research acceleration, massively parallel slow research, and the timely abandonment of things that do not work.

Both projects are starting with open documents and reusable templates. Bring a question, an example, or a correction.

## Manifesto

1. **Accelerate careful work.** Automate repetition, share useful tools, and make time to understand the problem. Higher research quality remains the goal.
2. **Scale slow research through collaboration.** Share code, proofs, experiments, and failed attempts. Let others inspect and build on the work.
3. **Measure what matters.** Record assumptions, costs, and results. A fast pipeline still needs a sound question and evidence that holds up.
4. **Make stopping productive.** State what would change your mind. When a route stops working, document why and make room for the next one.
5. **Keep the community civil.** Discuss ideas, welcome disagreement, and offer specific feedback. No personal attacks, spam, or invented results.

Slow research is a long-term undertaking. Time is short; there is much to do.

## Join the working group

Open an issue in either project with a tool, a research bottleneck, a useful template, or a failed approach worth documenting. Small, concrete contributions are welcome.
